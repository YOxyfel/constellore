Privacy notice

Effective 22 July 2026. This notice covers the free Constellore browser beta operated by Yane Zhekov under the studio name Oxyfel Games in Bulgaria.

The short version

You can play without giving us a name, email address, password, or payment details. Solo progress stays on your device. If you deliberately enter a live Scramble match, its pairings, connection state, result, and separate Duel Rating are processed through a pseudonymous guest profile so both players can share the same race.

Who is responsible

The beta is operated by Yane Zhekov, an individual in Bulgaria using the studio name Oxyfel Games. The official project website is yoxyfel.github.io/constellore. Until a private support mailbox is activated, non-sensitive questions can be submitted through the public beta issue tracker. Never put a Recovery Kit, bearer token, address, payment information, or other private information in a public issue.

Local practice

The GitHub Pages and itch.io editions keep solo discoveries, settings, progression, and bounded diagnostic counters in browser storage on your device. They do not upload solo gameplay analytics, solo scores, or custom targets. Live Scramble is optional and clearly requires a network connection; opening it creates or reuses a pseudonymous Duel identity and sends only the live-match requests described below. If you deliberately send a missing-combination idea, the game saves a local copy and sends only the two words, one optional short result, one fixed reason, the game mode, and a random duplicate-check identifier to the Constellore feedback service hosted on Cloudflare Workers and D1. It asks for no name, email, or open comment. The raw identifier and your IP address are not stored by Oxyfel Games: the identifier becomes a pair-scoped one-way digest, while one-way reporter and short-lived network buckets enforce abuse limits. Inactive report rows expire after 180 days. Cloudflare necessarily processes ordinary network metadata to deliver and rate-limit the request. If delivery is unavailable, the idea stays in a local retry queue and the interface says so. A service worker may cache game files for offline play. You can erase the local information from Menu → Settings and data → Privacy and data or by clearing the site’s storage.

Optional online beta and live Scramble

If you use the separately hosted online beta or open Scramble from a portable build, the service may store a generated guest identifier and callsign, Duel invitations and matchmaking state, every pairing attempted during a live match, the resulting open boards, connection and reconnect events, match results, seasonal Duel Rating changes, verified run records, leaderboard entries, recovery-code and session verification records, aggregate analytics, recipe ratings, bounded missing-combination reports, launch-interest status, and security/rate-limit records. During a Scramble, both competitors can see the other player's callsign, attempts, successes, failures, and discovered route; the interface does not expose internal account IDs or bearer credentials. Solo gameplay words, Stardust, Route Rank, streaks, and settings stay on your device in the portable release; cloud-profile saving is disabled.

Active live-match state and completed Duel results are retained for reconnect, rating integrity, repeat-pair limits, review, and deletion/export controls. Finished in-memory lobbies expire, while bounded result and rating ledgers remain until the online identity is erased or the beta is reset. Gameplay diagnostics are restricted to an allowlist and aggregated. Daily operational analytics are retained for up to 90 days. Server backups exclude bearer and recovery secrets and rotate according to the host configuration.

Why information is used

  • To provide the run, save progress, recover a pseudonymous profile, and verify a result you request.
  • To protect leaderboards and the service against automated abuse.
  • To understand whether onboarding and combinations work using bounded aggregate diagnostics.
  • To investigate a recipe rating or a deliberately submitted “expected this to combine” report.

The production legal-basis assessment, processor list, and private contact route must be reviewed before accounts, advertising, or payments expand beyond this free beta.

Experimental AI

Ranked recipes never depend on live AI. If experimental online generation is enabled and you deliberately request it, the two concepts and a bounded amount of recent game context may be sent to the configured AI provider. The public Pages and itch Explore mode is deterministic and sends nothing to an AI provider. Do not enter names, contact details, confidential information, or other personal data as a word. Generated recipes are provisional, may be wrong, and can be quarantined or removed.

Your controls

  • Export the local or online profile from Menu → Settings and data.
  • Erase the profile through the Account section’s in-game deletion control.
  • Reset local diagnostics and preferences from the Privacy and data section on the same device.
  • Stop using the online profile and clear local site data at any time.

Because the current account is deliberately pseudonymous, the Recovery Kit is the strongest proof that a person controls a profile. Never send the kit to anyone.

Children, payments, and changes

The beta is not designed to collect children’s personal information and currently contains no checkout, paid entitlement, personalized advertising, or rewarded advertising. A final age-position and Bulgarian/EU professional review are required before commercial release. Material changes will be dated here and reflected in the game’s release notes.
