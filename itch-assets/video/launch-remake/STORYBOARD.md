# Launch Remake Storyboard

## Story spine

Past damage is not discarded; it becomes navigation, strength, and a new universe.

1. **Ignition — Lessons become fuel.** Gold leaves the cracks of ruined relics and converges beneath the ship.
2. **Atmospheric break — The fracture becomes a road.** The ship turns a split in the storm into its ascent path.
3. **Planetary flight — Earth becomes the first slingshot.** The ship races around Earth and between other planets while a distant black hole establishes its destination.
4. **Black-hole entry — The point of no return.** The destination grows to overwhelming scale as the ship crosses the event horizon.
5. **Singularity approach — Motion becomes inevitability.** Inside the black hole, warped light collapses toward one small central singularity.

## Visual continuity

- One original hero ship throughout: narrow spearhead silhouette, split crescent fins, faceted black-obsidian hull, recessed champagne-gold repair seams, and one circular cyan-white fusion core.
- Premium photorealistic 3D cinematic with physically based materials, path-traced detail, volumetric atmosphere, restrained anamorphic highlights, and subtle film grain.
- Palette: obsidian black, charcoal stone, antique champagne gold, warm white, deep navy, restrained cyan and violet.
- No text, logo, watermark, UI, border, people, or recognizable franchise design.

## Suggested motion and edit

### Shot 1 — 0:00–0:02.2

Slow ground-level camera push along the central gold fissure. Energy travels from the statues into the launch cradle. Dust rises before the engines bloom. Hold the ship silhouette for several frames before liftoff.

### Shot 2 — 0:02.2–0:04.2

Cut on engine bloom to a close rear chase camera. The ship accelerates through rain and cloud layers. The cloud rupture propagates as a branching gold seam, then the camera rolls slightly as stars appear.

### Shot 3 — 0:04.2–0:06.8

Match the roll into a wide rear tracking shot above Earth. The ship banks around the planet at extreme speed, then threads between progressively more distant planets and moons. Keep the black hole small at the vanishing point so the destination is understood before it becomes dangerous.

### Shot 4 — 0:06.8–0:09.0

Use the distant black hole as the match-cut anchor and grow it rapidly until it dominates the frame. Planets fall away behind the ship. Gravitational lensing bends the star field and accretion disk around the dark center as the craft crosses the event horizon.

### Shot 5 — 0:09.0–0:12.0

Cut from the exterior event horizon to a near-black interior. Keep the ship centered and reduce apparent acceleration as if time is dilating. Curved remnants of starlight converge on one tiny white-gold singularity. End on the approach without revealing what lies beyond.

### Shot 1 prompt

A monumental launch cathedral of cracked dark stone and ancient ruined statues. Champagne-gold Kintsugi seams race across the floor from the relics and converge beneath the centered ship, visibly turning accumulated history into propulsion. First instant of ignition. Low ground-level 24 mm camera with the foreground fissure as a leading line. Solemn anticipation, near-black atmosphere, warm gold and cold cyan-white engine light.

### Shot 2 prompt

The same ship violently breaks through a colossal storm ceiling. Its engines carve a vertical path while champagne-gold light stitches the cloud rupture behind it, turning the fracture into a road forward. Fast low rear three-quarter chase camera, 28 mm lens, turbulent cloud walls, rain, stone fragments, planetary curve, and first stars. Cathartic upward momentum.

### Shot 3 prompt

The same ship slingshots around an unmistakable Earth at extreme hypersonic visual speed. Earth fills much of the lower frame with blue oceans, clouds, atmospheric rim, and strong curvature. The craft threads between separated planets and moons receding through depth. A small but unmistakable black hole with a thin gold-white accretion ring waits at the far vanishing point. Dynamic rear three-quarter tracking shot, sharp ship and planets, compressed star streaks at the edges.

### Shot 4 prompt

The same ship makes its final high-speed approach to the black hole. The event horizon is enormous and physically convincing: a perfectly dark center, thin brilliant gold-white accretion disk, strong lensing above and below, and distorted stars. The ship is aimed directly at the center and is crossing the threshold. Wide rear chase shot, ship in the lower central third, tiny planets left far behind.

### Shot 5 prompt

Inside the black hole, the same ship approaches a very small, impossibly intense white-gold singularity at the exact central vanishing point. Outside planets and the accretion disk are gone. Distorted starlight, restrained blue-violet photon arcs, and thin gold-white filaments curve inward through immense dark negative space. Symmetrical rear tracking shot, ship smaller in the lower central third, eerie apparent slowing from time dilation, ending before contact.
