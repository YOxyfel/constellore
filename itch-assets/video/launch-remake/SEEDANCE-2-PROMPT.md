## Paste-ready prompt

Create a **15-second, 16:9 cinematic game-intro animation in one single uninterrupted take**. Use `@Image 1` through `@Image 5` as chronological visual waypoints along the same continuous camera move. Preserve the exact same hero ship throughout: narrow spearhead silhouette, split crescent fins, faceted black-obsidian hull, restrained champagne-gold seams, circular cyan-white rear core, and twin white-gold engines. Use `@Video 1` only as reference for the original launch-to-black-hole journey, its accelerating rhythm, and the final transition into complete black.

**Absolute camera rule:** the camera never cuts, never changes to another shot, never teleports, and never loses the ship. It begins low behind the ship and continuously follows it with one smooth stabilized chase-camera move. Every environment change must happen naturally through forward travel, coherent parallax, camera rotation, and speed ramps. The timed beats below are positions inside one unbroken shot, never edits.

**0.0–2.4 seconds — ignition and liftoff:** Begin very low behind the ship inside the monumental cracked dark-stone launch cathedral shown in `@Image 1`. Champagne-gold fissures lead toward the launch cradle. Engines compress into a bright white-gold core, dust and vapor roll backward, and the ship lifts off. The camera accelerates smoothly with it, staying close behind and slightly below. Preserve realistic mass and engine force.

**2.4–4.8 seconds — atmospheric escape:** Without cutting, follow the ship upward through the opening above the cathedral and into the storm from `@Image 2`. The ship punches through rain, dense clouds, and the upper atmosphere. Use a controlled speed ramp: normal acceleration becomes violently fast as the craft breaks the cloud ceiling, producing a powerful cinematic sonic-boom shockwave and a gold-white vapor cone. The camera remains locked behind the ship while rolling gently with its climb. Earth’s curvature and stars appear naturally ahead.

**4.8–9.0 seconds — Earth slingshot and planetary warp flight:** The same continuous camera follows as the ship banks around Earth from `@Image 3`. Earth is large and unmistakable below, with blue oceans, clouds, atmospheric glow, and strong curvature. The craft accelerates to extreme warp-like speed. Stars stretch into long directional lines; light compresses around the frame edges; the engine trail becomes a white-gold ribbon. The ship flies between several separated planets and moons with coherent scale and parallax, never colliding and never teleporting. Planets whip past on alternating depths while the camera smoothly rolls and banks with the ship. A small black hole with a thin luminous accretion ring remains visible far ahead and grows continuously as the destination.

**9.0–12.2 seconds — black-hole approach and entry:** Still without a cut, the planets recede behind and the black hole grows to the overwhelming scale shown in `@Image 4`. Depict a physically convincing perfectly dark event horizon, a brilliant thin gold-white accretion disk, gravitational lensing above and below, and stars bending around it. Increase speed again as the ship commits to the center. The camera stays directly behind the craft and follows it across the event horizon. The accretion disk and outside universe stretch around the edges and disappear naturally; do not use a flash, wipe, or portal transition.

**12.2–15.0 seconds — interior singularity and menu transition:** Continue the same camera path inside the black hole, matching the near-black warped interior of `@Image 5`. Outside planets and the accretion disk are gone. Faint blue-violet photon arcs and thin gold-white light filaments curve inward toward one tiny white-gold singularity exactly at the center. Time dilation makes the camera’s tracking speed gradually lag while the ship continues forward, so the ship becomes smaller and smaller without a cut, finally reducing to a single centered point of light. At the same time, the circular black-hole shadow expands smoothly outward from the center until it completely engulfs every edge of the screen. End on **uniform absolute black with no residual stars, glow, vignette, or texture**, and hold pure black for the final 8–12 frames so the main menu can appear seamlessly.

**Visual style:** premium photorealistic 3D cinematic animation; physically based materials; high-end game-studio trailer; realistic planetary scale and gravitational optics; controlled dark-fantasy prestige; obsidian black, champagne gold, warm white, deep navy, restrained cyan and violet; volumetric atmosphere; subtle film grain; restrained anamorphic highlights; smooth cinematic motion blur.

**Sound design:** synchronized stereo audio with no dialogue or voiceover. Deep engine ignition and stone-rumble at launch; rain and air pressure building into one clean sonic boom; rising warp tone and Doppler-like pass energy during planetary flight; immense low gravitational bass and stretched metallic resonance near the event horizon; inside the black hole, sound elongates and loses high frequencies; the final point collapses into a soft sub-bass pulse followed by complete silence during the pure-black hold.

**Hard constraints:** one ship only; never redesign or morph the ship; no cockpit view; no extra spacecraft; no cuts; no montage; no shot changes; no camera teleportation; no sudden location jumps; no duplicated planets; no planet collisions; no ruins or relic fields in space; no constellation-map graphics; no artificial portal ring; no city; no text; no title; no logo; no watermark; no UI; no border; no resemblance to an existing science-fiction franchise; no white flash at the black-hole entry; no hard cut to black.
