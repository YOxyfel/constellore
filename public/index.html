    1. VERSION 5.0.0-BETA.4AUG 12, 2026LATEST

      Continuous Deep Space

      Earth and Moon now remain on one stable physical pivot while zooming through every precision rebase. Their authored close views blend into the astronomical chart at the same position and apparent size, and visiting a body keeps its camera fit while the represented distance changes.

      Solar, nearby-star, deep-sky, Milky Way, Local Group, and cosmic structures now prewarm and emerge across long logarithmic crossfades. Constellation paths and cosmic filaments draw progressively instead of appearing as finished geometry in one frame.

      The far chart now uses collision-aware stellar labels and one boundless, distance-faded world grid that retunes its metric scale without swapping planes. Solid bodies—including the Sun—shrink by their physical projection; once the Sun becomes sub-pixel, only its additive light and glare remain.

      The Milky Way continues outward through real-scale galaxy groups, superclusters, the cosmic web, and the observable universe. The complete optional atlas remains outside the interaction-critical bundle.

    2. VERSION 5.0.0-BETA.1JUL 29, 2026

      Scramble Arena

      Live 1v1 has arrived. Invite a friend to a private race or enter public matchmaking after your first scored solo win. Both players receive the same authored target and starting words, then launch together after a three-second countdown.

      Every pairing is open knowledge. You can watch the rival attempt, fail, discover, steal, and change routes in real time, then manually race to use what you learned. There is no copy button and no hidden board.

      The first player to make the target wins. After five minutes, the player closer to the verified route wins; an exact tie is a draw. Private matches never affect rating, while public matches use a separate seasonal 1v1 Rating and cannot change solo Route Rank, Stardust, mastery, or events.

      Desktop and tablet keep both boards visible. Phones keep your own board primary with a permanent rival ticker and a full rival-board toggle. Refresh rejoins an active match, brief connection loss receives a recovery window, and completed matches support rematch and sharing.

      Pages and itch remain fully local and offline for solo play. Only Scramble uses the live service, and its extra client files load on demand. Cosmos Circuit remains disabled while it receives separate development.

    3. VERSION 4.0.0-BETA.4JUL 29, 2026

      Shape Your Constellation

      Landscape launches now begin with the full 24-second “A Production by Oxyfel Games” Kintsugi ident, followed by the spaceship film through a fade-to-black transition and continuous cinematic sound design. The dedicated portrait phone cut is intentionally unchanged.

      The full-screen opening begins automatically on page load. If a browser refuses first-visit sound autoplay, the film continues immediately in muted mode instead of waiting for a click, and sound remains available from its control.

      The opening plays once per browser session. Refreshing Home returns directly to Home, while refreshing during a game restores the saved target, moves, discoveries, board positions, hints, and correct run music without replaying either intro.

      Quit game now returns Home in one click without another confirmation or a Cosmic Gate delay. Direct master-volume decrease, percentage, and increase controls remain visible in every word level and Pause, with the full Music and Effects mixer still available beside them.

      Each successful combination now grows a small story scene directly on the board: the first result becomes its foundation, later discoveries add landscape, life, structure, energy, and sky layers, and a wrong pair crumbles then rebuilds the valid story without deleting progress. Used hints stay visible as a Current Hint objective.

      Star Trail now begins with three stars and scales to four and five. Every memory round locks input while showing the numbered order for exactly five seconds, then hides the numbers before recall. Completed games use automatic starting words without an extra selector, and Silver's Explore catalog now sits clearly beneath the primary orbit.

      The Cosmetic Lab presents the equipped look live and lets players switch any unlocked piece directly from their inventory. Eight complete collections include responsive Home and opening Gate backgrounds; Pixel Frontier, Bubble Reef, Stellar Vanguard, Lunar Garden, and Eclipse Sovereign each retain their distinct style.

      Cosmetics remain visual-only. Locked looks can be previewed, earned pieces can be mixed into any collection, effect intensity can be reduced or disabled, and responsive collection art now loads on demand without increasing the base install.

    4. VERSION 3.5.1-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      Your Ideas Can Reach Us

      Missing-combination ideas can now use a free, anonymous feedback receiver instead of requiring GitHub. The game sends only the two released words, one optional short answer, a fixed reason, the mode, and a duplicate-check value.

      Every idea is still saved locally first and temporary failures remain in the small offline retry queue. The receiver stores neither a raw player identifier nor an IP address, and inactive rows expire automatically.

    5. VERSION 3.5.0-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      First Paths, Shared Skies

      The opening personal challenges now draw from a hand-tuned Golden 50: familiar targets with plain clues, verified three-to-seven-combination routes, and multiple logical ways to make the final word.

      Every completed route can become a contextual, spoiler-safe constellation card. Its colors, celestial motif, clue, universe, Daily identity, and route silhouette belong to that exact journey, while its link preserves the target and seed for the next player.

      The first ten completed games now stay focused on Play, combine, celebrate, and continue. Sharing, game choices, and Explore arrive in small steps; advanced ranks, competition, mastery, and economy surfaces wait until the core game is familiar.

    6. VERSION 3.4.8-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      A Clearer Finish

      More details is now about half its former height. It keeps the useful path, compact route grade, reward, scoring consequences, and sharing while removing duplicate metric boxes, the repeated mini-route, and the unavailable Community Sky explanation from local practice.

      Completed Daily games and the final Weekly stage now offer Play next level and open a fresh relaxed challenge. Normal adaptive wins, Weekly stages, stories, onboarding, answer playback, and safe score uploads keep their purpose-built next actions.

    7. VERSION 3.4.7-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      A Gentle First Light

      A new player's first target is Mud: combine Earth and Water once, enjoy the discovery, and win with no clock or move limit. Bronze and Silver keep that relaxed spirit with no clock or move limit; timed and limited-move games unlock at Gold.

      Ending a personal game without the target now opens a different, gentler challenge instead of repeating the same frustrating level. Replaying that exact challenge remains a deliberate secondary choice.

      Challenge adjustment stays behind the scenes. Constellore never exposes hidden levels, failure counters, or formulas; only an exceptional run may show the simple Difficult label.

    8. VERSION 3.4.6-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      Play now begins the chosen game in one tap. The Cosmic Gate closes immediately, prepares the challenge behind the doors, and opens directly onto the ready board.

      Timed runs, scoring, and competitive progress remain frozen while the objective is open. They begin only when Start is pressed, so reading the goal never costs time or points.

    9. VERSION 3.4.5-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      Launch and new-level Cosmic Gates now pause long enough to show one of 50 short thoughts about imagination, creation, and discovery. The game avoids an immediate repeat and gives a clear author credit to each historical line.

      The first screen now explains the target in simpler language and cycles through six logical example recipes, including Species + Air → Bird and Brick + Brick → Wall, instead of leaving one pair on screen forever.

    10. VERSION 3.4.4-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      Little Games Between Worlds

      Some completed personal challenges now lead to a short, optional Star Break before the next word. Connect four matching star pairs without crossing their constellation paths, or tap a trail of stars in order to light a new shape.

      Star Breaks are calm breaks, not tests. They have no timer and never change points, rewards, Route Rank, leaderboards, Run IQ, or challenge difficulty. Skip is always available.

      Each puzzle is generated from a checked solution and works with touch, mouse, or keyboard. Matching symbols support the colors, large targets make phone play comfortable, and reduced-motion and high-contrast settings are respected.

    11. VERSION 3.4.3-BETA.1JUL 26, 2026

      A New Sky Between Worlds

      The main menu now has its own constellation-world panorama instead of borrowing the Cosmic Gate artwork. Floating celestial islands, star rivers, and a quiet center give the home screen a distinct place in the universe.

      Portrait, 1080p, and 4K-sized menu sources keep the scene crisp while each device loads only the artwork suited to its screen.

      After a mission is confirmed, the Cosmic Gate now closes over the menu, prepares the board while hidden, and reopens directly onto the words. Timed challenges wait until the board is visible before their countdown begins.

      The launch gate now keeps only the CONSTELLORE name on screen, removing the short-lived extra message instead of making every launch slower.

      The home background is now anchored to the viewport, so its panorama remains continuous while the page grows or scrolls instead of drifting with the content.

      If a verified score is still waiting to be saved, the result stays visible until it is safe to leave. Pending progress can no longer disappear behind a closed panel.

    14. VERSION 3.4.0-BETA.1JUL 25, 2026

      The Cosmic Gate Opens

      Constellore now opens on a living cosmos. A bright line draws through its center, the sky separates into two parallax doors, and the universe opens onto a cleaner, richer main menu with one obvious Play action.

      The same doors now close around Pause and every completed game. Pause keeps Continue, Restart, Show path, and Quit together in one calm panel; Show path clearly warns that it gives no points before the answer begins.

      Show Answer now grows the solution upward like constellation roots. All currently reachable branches animate in parallel, each result continues into the next combination, and Completed words and links remain as gently dimmed history behind the bright active wave. Pause, speed, finish, and one replay stay available; Study still gives no score, rewards, or saved reveal discoveries.

      Challenge matching now stays private. The game no longer shows hidden levels, formulas, streak rules, or future adjustments. Only an especially hard mission receives one plain Difficult tag.

    16. VERSION 3.2.0-BETA.1JUL 25, 2026

      A Journey That Learns How You Play

      Run IQ now begins at 0. Discoveries that genuinely move you toward the target earn most of the points, unrelated discoveries earn only a little, and a flawless target route can finish at 200. Experimenting with word pairs never secretly changes the current challenge.

      Rewards match the target that was actually chosen, recently played targets are avoided, and every adaptive target has a checked route from its exact starting words.
