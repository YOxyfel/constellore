Dev Logs / Updates

See what changed in each version.

  1. VERSION 3.3.0-BETA.1JUL 25, 2026LATEST

    One Play, a Growing Universe

    The main Play button now chooses a suitable challenge and opening for you. Bronze begins with Earth, Water, Fire, and Air; new mixes arrive gently from Silver onward, lending useful temporary words from a checked part of the real answer route. After a win, choose whether the next challenge uses Auto, the same four words, or a new mix.

    Twelve permanent Route Ranks grow through mastery and best-of-three promotion games. Higher ranks combine more Route Remixes, while adaptive intensity adds unfamiliar rules only after you have shown you are ready. An easier challenge never removes a rank you already earned.

    Six board skies now evolve with your rank, from a quiet common night through living nebulae and astral rifts to the crowned singularity for Legend and Cosmic. Responsive mobile, balanced, and cinematic artwork keeps every device light and readable.

    Every adaptive target and starting mix is checked for a real route, with 500 possible destination words and recent targets avoided. These personal games do not enter shared leaderboards; Daily, Weekly, friend, story, training, and player-chosen challenges stay fixed for everyone.

  2. VERSION 3.2.0-BETA.1JUL 25, 2026

    A Journey That Learns How You Play

    Normal Relaxed, Timed, and Limited Moves games now choose a reachable target that fits your recent games. Every three completed challenges raise your normal level. One unfinished challenge lowers it by one. Every tenth completion unlocks one much harder Surge challenge. Missing it keeps your old level safe; finishing it unassisted with no mistakes and 200 Run IQ makes that Surge level your new normal.

    Run IQ now begins at 0. Discoveries that genuinely move you toward the target earn most of the points, unrelated discoveries earn only a little, and a flawless target route can finish at 200. Trying a word pair that does not combine never changes challenge difficulty.

    Rewards match the target that was actually chosen, recently played targets are avoided, and every adaptive target has a checked route from its exact starting words.

    These are personal games, so they do not enter shared leaderboards. Daily, Weekly, friend, story, training, and player-chosen targets stay exactly the same for everyone.

  3. VERSION 3.1.0-BETA.1JUL 24, 2026

    Clearer Play, Better Answers

    Constellore 3.1 keeps one obvious action in focus, gives the game a warmer illustrated identity, and places Undo, Redo, Tidy, and Clear directly on the board. Larger text, familiar words, calmer menus, and clearer player feedback make every mode easier to understand.

    Find-the-word modes now show honest steps remaining and a playful Run IQ score. New valid pairs build a multiplier up to ×3, misses cost only two points, repeats cannot be farmed, and 200 is the cap. The authored universe has grown from 1,066 to 3,199 logical pairs without changing ranked rules.

    Show Answer now performs one readable fusion at a time: two words meet, the result appears with light trails and a discovery burst, and the route advances without overlapping copies. It still disables scoring, allows one replay, and then returns to the main screen.

  4. VERSION 3.0.0-BETA.2JUL 23, 2026

    The Path Becomes the Game

    A calmer First Orbit now teaches one visible destination before the rest of the universe unfolds. World Graph 3 adds a faster sparse runtime, hundreds of reviewed logical connections, ninety Daily challenge variants, one honest Guidance ladder, a live constellation trail, clearer Pure/Open/Practice/Study rules, privacy-safe missing-pair feedback, stronger ranked-run integrity, and the beta.2 compatibility fix for the Living Atlas.

    Pages and itch are deterministic local practice without live rankings, accounts, or AI. Beta progress may reset.

  5. VERSION 2.1.0-BETA.1JUL 22, 2026

    Signature Constellations

    Completed routes now earn a Signature grade for efficiency, discovery, originality, and fair play. The Living Atlas draws every performed recipe as a real star map; connected Voyages offer curated destination chains; successful recipes explain their logic; failed pairs can provide spoiler-safe directional clues; verified completions gain asynchronous community comparisons; and weekly Cosmic Events add themed targets and collectible discoveries without changing canonical recipes.

    Pages and itch are deterministic local practice without live rankings, accounts, or AI. Beta progress may reset.

  6. VERSION 2.0.0-BETA.1JUL 22, 2026

    The Living Atlas

    The catalog now holds 770 authored connections; 679 are naturally reachable from the four starters, producing 600+ distinct reachable results and 110+ same-word evolutions. Every mission starts with a clear target brief; help uses visible Open score rates; and new mastery, badges, momentum, diagnostics, accessibility, and release safeguards make every orbit easier to understand. Known beta limit: Pages and itch are deterministic local practice without live rankings, accounts, or AI. Beta progress may reset.

  7. UPDATE 03JUL 21, 2026

    Route Signals & Smoother Stamping

    Route Signals now guide you toward the next valid connection without revealing the answer or costing score. Hold SHIFT before or during a drag—even straight from Your universe—to stamp evenly spaced copies. Board messages now queue in one clear lane, so recipes, mastery, and control feedback no longer overlap.

  8. UPDATE 02JUL 21, 2026

    Shift Board Tools

    SHIFT over a board word clears it when your hand is empty. Hold Shift before or during any drag to leave evenly spaced copies; drop the held word onto another to fuse.

  9. UPDATE 01JUL 18, 2026

    Ctrl Hover Fusion

    Hold CTRL, hover one board word, then sweep onto another to combine instantly. The last result stays ready, making long discovery chains flow without extra clicks.

    Shipped with the release and recorded separately as the first gameplay update.
  10. PUBLIC BETA RELEASEJUL 16, 2026

    First Light

    Constellore opened its public browser beta: begin with four elements, build a constellation of discoveries, and find the target word across Reach, Quick Orbit, Move Limit, and daily play.
