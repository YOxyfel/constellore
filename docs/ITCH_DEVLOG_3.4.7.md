# Constellore 3.4.7 - A Gentle First Light

The universe should invite you in before it tests you.

## Your first win is one fusion away

A new player's first target is now **Mud**. Bring Earth and Water together and the board answers immediately—no clock, no move limit, and no long recipe to memorize.

That small first success teaches the whole idea of Constellore in one satisfying moment: combine two words, discover something new, and reach the target.

## Early journeys are made for exploring

Bronze and Silver personal challenges are now untimed and have unlimited moves. Players can move words around, test ideas, and enjoy discoveries without being rushed.

Timed and limited-move challenges arrive from Gold onward, after the basic rhythm already feels natural.

## A loss opens a new path

When a personal challenge ends without the target, **Next** now creates a different, gentler journey. It will not quietly repeat the word that just caused frustration.

Players who genuinely want the exact same puzzle can still choose replay as a secondary action. The normal path always moves forward.

Difficulty adjustment remains behind the scenes. Constellore does not show hidden levels, failure counters, or formulas; only an exceptional challenge may carry the simple **Difficult** label.

The itch.io HTML5 build remains deterministic local practice. It does not currently include live accounts, shared leaderboards, payments, or live AI. Progress is stored in your browser on this device and may reset during beta development.
