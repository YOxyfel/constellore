# Constellore 3.4.5 - A Thought Between Worlds

Every new journey now begins with a moment to look up.

## Fifty thoughts at the Cosmic Gate

The opening doors now move more slowly on launch and before a new word challenge. While the cosmos is closed, one of 50 short thoughts appears—small invitations to imagine, discover, and make something that did not exist a moment ago.

Most were written for Constellore. A small selection comes from well-known historical writers and explorers, with their names and sources kept alongside the text. The game avoids showing the same line twice in a row.

Pause and Escape have not been slowed down. They remain immediate controls.

## A clearer first screen

The opening message now explains the game in simpler language: you are given a target word, and every combination is one step toward making it.

The example recipe also changes as you watch. Instead of always showing the same Earth + Water combination, it moves through several familiar, logical discoveries so the idea becomes clear before the first game starts.

## Still built for every screen

Quotes stay short, large, and centered across phones, tablets, and desktop screens. Reduced-motion settings remove the sweeping movement while keeping the words readable, and the game timer still waits until the board is fully revealed.

The itch.io HTML5 build remains deterministic local practice. It does not currently include live accounts, shared leaderboards, payments, or live AI. Progress is stored in your browser on this device and may reset during beta development.
