# Constellore 3.4.4 - Little Games Between Worlds

Constellore's word journeys now have an occasional change of pace: a very short Star Break can appear after some completed personal challenges.

## Connect a constellation

One Star Break places four matching pairs around a small night sky. Connect every pair with a constellation path, but do not let the paths cross.

The matching stars use symbols as well as colors, and every generated board begins with a checked solution. It works with touch, mouse, or keyboard.

## Trace a star trail

The second Star Break is even simpler: tap the numbered stars in order and watch the constellation light up behind you.

There is no timer and no punishment for taking your time.

## A break, not another grind

Star Breaks are optional breathers. They never change:

- points or Run IQ;
- rewards or progress;
- Route Rank or challenge difficulty;
- leaderboard results.

Skip is always available, and either finishing or skipping leads straight to the next word challenge. The game spaces these breaks several completed challenges apart so they stay fresh instead of interrupting the main loop.

The interface respects reduced-motion, increased-contrast, and forced-color settings, with large targets designed for phones and tablets.

## Beta note

The itch.io HTML5 build is deterministic local practice. It does not currently include live accounts, shared leaderboards, payments, or live AI. Progress is stored in your browser on this device and may reset during beta development.
