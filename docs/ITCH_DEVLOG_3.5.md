# First Paths, Shared Skies — Update 3.5

Constellore's opening journeys are now more carefully authored, easier to understand, and much better to share.

## A Golden 50 for early play

The first thirty personal challenges now prefer a hand-tuned set of fifty familiar destinations.

Every Golden target includes:

- a verified authored route of three to seven combinations;
- a concise clue written in plain language;
- at least two logical recipes that can create the final word;
- the same behavior in the browser, Pages, and itch.io builds.

Daily, Weekly, friend, story, and player-chosen challenges keep their fixed targets.

## Every path gets its own sky

Completed routes can now become contextual constellation cards. The card's palette, celestial motif, clue, universe, Cosmic Law, Daily identity, and constellation silhouette are selected from the actual challenge and route.

Recipe words remain hidden, so sharing a beautiful card never gives away the answer. The invitation preserves the exact target and seed. A link to today's word opens the real shared Daily; an older or already-completed Daily becomes a replayable friend challenge instead of expiring.

Native sharing includes the card image where the device supports it. The fallback copies a complete invitation and direct link in one action.

## Ten calm games before the larger universe

The opening progression now stays centered on the essential loop: Play, combine, celebrate, and continue.

- Daily follows the two short lessons.
- Sharing opens after the first real win.
- More game choices open after two wins.
- Explore opens after three wins.
- Weekly adventures, leaderboards, mastery, the Word Exchange, supporter options, Run IQ, Rival Ghosts, recipe surveys, and progression-heavy results wait until ten completed games.

Settings, privacy, help consequences, path viewing, and sharing remain available from the beginning.

This update is about making the first hour feel like a game rather than a dashboard—and making every successful route feel worth sending to someone else. ✦
