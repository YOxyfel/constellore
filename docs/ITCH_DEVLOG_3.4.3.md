# Constellore 3.4.3 — A New Sky Between Worlds

The main menu has a universe of its own, and pressing Play now feels like travelling from that universe onto the game board.

## A new home among the stars

The main menu no longer reuses the blue-and-gold Cosmic Gate. It now opens onto a quieter constellation world filled with celestial islands, flowing star paths, distant planets, and a clear center that keeps Play easy to find.

The background has separate portrait, 1080p, and 4K-sized versions. Phones receive the tall composition, ordinary landscape screens receive the lighter 1080p source, and large or high-density displays receive the more detailed landscape source.

## Play passes through the gate

Choosing a mode still shows the short mission summary first. Once the mission is confirmed:

1. the Cosmic Gate closes over the main menu;
2. the game prepares the board while the doors are shut;
3. the doors reopen directly onto the playable words.

This transition is shared by normal games, First Orbit, Second Orbit, Explore, and direct main-menu starts. Restored games and retries do not replay it unnecessarily.

Timed challenges now begin counting down only after the doors have reopened, so the animation never costs the player time.

The opening itself is cleaner too: only the **CONSTELLORE** name remains on screen. The brief extra lines were removed instead of slowing down every launch just to make them readable.

## Beta note

The itch.io HTML5 build is deterministic local practice. It does not currently include live accounts, shared leaderboards, payments, or live AI. Progress is stored in your browser on this device and may reset during beta development.
