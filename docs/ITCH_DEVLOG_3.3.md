# Constellore 3.3 — One Play, a Growing Universe

Constellore 3.3 is here. This update makes every game easier to begin, more rewarding to master, and more beautiful as you progress.

Press **Play** and the game now chooses a suitable challenge and opening for you. Some games begin with the familiar elements—Earth, Water, Fire, and Air—while others lend you a new mix of temporary starting words. Every selected target is checked to make sure it can be reached from the exact words you receive.

## What’s new

- **500 possible target words**
- **12 permanent Route Ranks**, each with demanding promotion challenges
- **Route Remixes** that gradually introduce Waypoints, blocked shortcuts, special route rules, and longer chains
- **Adaptive difficulty** that climbs after proven success and becomes kinder after an unfinished challenge
- **Run IQ** that starts at 0 and grows toward 200 through useful combinations, streaks, and accurate play
- **Six evolving board skies**, from a quiet Bronze night to a spectacular Legend and Cosmic singularity
- **Responsive artwork** optimized separately for phones, tablets, and computers

New players can learn without being overwhelmed. Experienced players will encounter deeper routes, combined rules, harder promotions, and fresh starting-word mixes. A newly earned rank is permanent even when the next challenge becomes easier.

Your next word is waiting among the stars.

Thank you for playing the beta. Your feedback continues to shape Constellore.

## Beta note

The itch.io HTML5 build is deterministic local practice. It does not currently include live accounts, shared leaderboards, payments, or live AI. Progress is stored in your browser on this device and may reset during beta development.
