# A Clearer Finish — Update 3.4.8

Constellore's completed-game screen is now faster to understand and easier to leave.

## A smaller, more useful summary

**More details** is roughly half its former height. It now focuses on what matters:

- how you reached the target;
- your compact route grade;
- rewards and scoring consequences;
- the option to share the challenge.

Duplicate metric boxes and the repeated miniature path have been removed. Local and itch.io practice also no longer show a large Community Sky panel whose only purpose was to explain that online comparisons were unavailable.

## Every finished journey can continue

The normal Next challenge action was already available after personal games. This update closes the remaining gaps:

- completing the Word of the Day now offers **Play next level**;
- completing the final Weekly stage also offers **Play next level**;
- both continue into a fresh relaxed challenge instead of trying to replay a completed fixed event.

Training, story chapters, ordinary Weekly stages, answer playback, and score-saving errors retain their own clear next actions.

Thanks for helping make every part of Constellore simpler, calmer, and more enjoyable. ✦
