# Constellore 3.4 — The Cosmic Gate Opens

Constellore now feels like entering a universe, not opening a webpage.

## A cinematic arrival

The game opens on a layered blue-and-gold cosmos. A bright line draws from top to bottom, divides the sky, and becomes the seam between two celestial doors. The doors then open with horizontal parallax to reveal the new home screen.

That same visual language now follows the whole play loop:

- Pause closes the cosmos around your game before showing its controls.
- Winning closes the doors around the completed constellation before revealing the result.
- Reduced-motion players get a calm, fast transition without sweeping movement.
- Mobile and short landscape screens receive their own crop and layout.

The home screen has also been rebuilt around one obvious **Play** action. Alternate games remain available, but they no longer compete with the next thing most players want to do. The cosmic panorama, connected recipe example, restrained atlas details, larger text, and clear focus states bring back the art without bringing back the clutter.

## A better Pause menu

Pause now keeps the four useful choices together:

- **Continue**
- **Show path** — clearly marked **No points**
- **Restart**
- **Quit game**

Choosing Show path opens the normal confirmation before anything is revealed, so the scoring consequence is visible twice and cannot be mistaken for a free hint.

## Answers grow into constellations

**Show Answer** no longer demonstrates one isolated combination at a time. The solution grows upward across the board like constellation roots:

- every currently reachable branch begins together;
- related combinations continue in parallel;
- results become ingredients for the next layer;
- completed words and links remain as softly dimmed history;
- the active wave glows clearly until the branches meet at the target.

You can pause the demonstration, change its speed, finish it immediately, and replay it once. Show Answer still turns the game into **Study**: it gives no score, rewards, streak credit, or saved reveal discoveries.

## Difficulty stays out of the way

Personal challenge matching now stays private. Players do not need to learn hidden levels, formulas, streak rules, or predictions about the next game. Only an exceptionally demanding mission receives one plain **Difficult** tag. Shared Daily, Weekly, friend, story, training, and player-chosen challenges remain fixed for everyone.

Thank you for playing the beta. If a route feels confusing or a combination is missing, use the in-game report option—your feedback helps shape the universe.

## Beta note

The itch.io HTML5 build is deterministic local practice. It does not currently include live accounts, shared leaderboards, payments, or live AI. Progress is stored in your browser on this device and may reset during beta development.
